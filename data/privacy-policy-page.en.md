# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 16, 2026

Welcome to SD Style Lab (hereinafter referred to as "this site"). This site is an experimental website for organizing, displaying, and retrieving AI image style comparison records and is currently in the testing phase.

This Privacy Policy explains how this site handles information related to you when you visit this site, use third-party accounts to log in, browse image content, download restricted resources, or contact the maintainer.

If you do not agree with all or part of this policy, you may choose to stop visiting this site or stop using login-related features.

## 1. Scope of Application

This policy applies to this site's web pages, API interfaces directly related to this site, and foundational services used to provide this site's functionality.

This policy does not apply to the following situations:

- Third-party login services you use themselves, such as GitHub, Google, Microsoft
- Third-party infrastructure services themselves, such as Cloudflare, Supabase
- Other external websites, repositories, blogs, or platforms you visit through links from this site

For how these third-party services handle your information, please refer to their respective published privacy policies or service terms.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on how you use this site, we may process the following information:

### 2.1 Login and Account Identification Information

When you choose to log in with a third-party account, this site may receive and process basic information related to your account through Supabase Auth, including:

- User identifiers returned by third-party platforms
- Nickname, name, or public display name
- Avatar URL
- Email address
- In-site authentication session status and associated in-site user ID

This site will not directly obtain your third-party account password.

### 2.2 Session, Cookie, and Local Preference Information

To maintain login status and improve the basic experience, this site currently processes the following browser-side information:

- Login session cookies, used to maintain your authentication status
- `theme` preference cookie, as well as theme preference values saved locally in the browser, used to remember light or dark appearance
- Display preferences saved after login, used to decide whether to show corresponding content to you

This information is mainly used for feature implementation and is not used to serve you advertisements.

### 2.3 Access, Request, and Security Operation Information

When you visit this site or call related interfaces, the site deployment platform, authentication platform, object storage platform, or application service itself may record necessary technical information, such as:

- Access time
- Request paths and request results
- IP address
- Browser type, operating system, device information, User-Agent
- Error logs, debug logs, or security audit logs

This information is mainly used for site operation, security protection, troubleshooting, performance optimization, and preventing abuse.

### 2.4 Information You Actively Submit

If you actively contact the maintainer through the project repository, blog, public contact methods, or other channels, you may provide:

- Contact information
- Feedback content
- Problem description
- Other information you actively attach

Please do not actively submit sensitive personal information such as ID documents, addresses, phone numbers, or detailed financial information in public issues, public comments, or other public pages.

### 2.5 Content Access and Download Related Information

When you browse model detail pages, switch display preferences, request restricted resource downloads, or access content requiring authentication, this site may process:

- Whether you are currently logged in
- Model identifiers, page paths, or resource identifiers you request to access
- Minimum necessary authentication information related to access control

This processing is used to determine whether you can access corresponding content and to safely return page data or download content.

### 2.6 In-Site Displayed Content Itself

This site mainly displays AI images, model descriptions, style comparison results, workflow files, prompt-related information, and derived metadata generated, organized, or collected by the maintainer. Such content is usually not personal data directly submitted by ordinary visitors.

However, if you believe that in-site displayed content involves your personal information, portrait, privacy, reputation, or other legitimate rights and interests, please contact the maintainer in a timely manner.

## 3. How We Use This Information

We process the above information usually for the following purposes:

- Provide web access, account login, session maintenance, and basic functionality
- Identify logged-in users and provide corresponding access permissions
- Save your theme preferences and display preferences
- Display models, image thumbnails, related metadata, and restricted download content
- Conduct security protection, anomaly detection, anti-abuse, risk control, and troubleshooting
- Maintain, optimize, and improve this site's content organization, performance, and user experience
- Fulfill corresponding obligations when required by laws, regulations, regulatory requirements, or dispute resolution needs

As of the last updated date of this policy, this site has not actively integrated in-site advertising, payment functions, or third-party behavioral analysis SDKs for marketing purposes. If such features are added in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

## 4. How We Share, Entrust Processing, or Disclose Information

We will not sell your personal information to third parties.

In the following situations, relevant information may be processed by third parties or disclosed in accordance with the law:

### 4.1 Third-Party Services Necessary for Service Implementation

This site currently relies on the following basic capabilities:

- Supabase: Used for authentication, sessions, databases, and related data access control
- Cloudflare and related capabilities: Used for site deployment, object storage, resource distribution, and related network services
- GitHub, Google, Microsoft: Only participate in identity authentication when you actively choose the corresponding login method

These third parties may process part of your data based on the needs of providing their services. We will try to limit the scope of sharing to the minimum necessary for implementing functionality.

### 4.2 External Interactions You Initiate

If you contact the maintainer through the GitHub repository, blog, third-party platforms, or public contact methods, the relevant platforms will process the information you submit according to their own rules.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements or Rights Protection Needs

When required by laws and regulations, administrative or judicial authorities, or necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of this site, the maintainer, other users, or the public, we may disclose necessary information in accordance with the law.

## 5. Cookie and Similar Technologies

This site currently mainly uses the following technologies directly related to functionality:

- Login session cookies: Used to maintain login status, refresh sessions, and complete restricted function access
- Theme preference cookies: Used to remember your chosen light or dark theme
- Theme preference values in browser local storage: Used to improve the page initial rendering experience

You can clear cookies or local storage through browser settings, but doing so may cause you to log out, lose theme settings, or affect the normal use of some features.

## 6. Publicly Displayed Content and Access Control

One of the core purposes of this site is to publicly display AI image style test results and related organized content. Therefore, some model information, image variants, model descriptions, and page content on the site may be publicly displayed.

At the same time, this site also sets access restrictions on some features, such as:

- Login status identification
- Restricted content display preference control
- Some download capabilities or restricted resource access require identity verification first

This means that the scope of content you see may differ depending on your access status.

## 7. Information Retention Period

We will retain relevant information for the period necessary to achieve the processing purpose, unless laws and regulations require a longer retention time. Generally:

- Login session information will be saved according to the validity period of the authentication service and session mechanism, and will gradually expire after refresh, expiration, or logout
- Theme preference cookies are currently used to remember long-term display preferences, and the browser-side retention time may be up to about 1 year, unless you clear them in advance
- Display preferences will be retained during your continued use of the account until you modify, delete related data, or no longer use the relevant account
- The retention time of access logs, error logs, and security logs usually depends on the deployment platform, infrastructure service provider, or operation strategy
- In-site models, images, workflows, and related organized content, as project content assets, may be retained for a long time during the project's continued operation

When the retention period expires, or the processing purpose has been achieved and there is no legal requirement to continue retaining, we will delete or anonymize relevant data within a reasonable scope.

We will try to take reasonable and appropriate security measures to protect information security, including but not limited to:

You should properly safeguard your third-party accounts, devices, and browser environments, and avoid staying logged in for a long time in public environments.
